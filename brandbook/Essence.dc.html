Adal
Honest. Fair. Exact.

Adal means fair and honest: a deal that is clean and above board. It is the one promise an auction has to keep.

An auction house that can show its work.

Many people bid at the same moment, and the system still has to pick one winner and one price, every time. Adal lets the seller choose the format, then makes the rules visible: English, Dutch, sealed first-price or Vickrey, all on one engine.

ExactTies, reserves and closing times resolve the same way every time.
OpenEvery rule is stated before the user bids, never after.
GuardedA fresh authenticator code confirms every purchase. A stolen session is not enough.
ImmediateNew bids reach every watcher within a second, without a refresh.
