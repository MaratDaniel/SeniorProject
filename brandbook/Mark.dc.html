Name. Adal means fair and honest. In an auction, fairness is balance: the same rules, the same clock and the same weight for every bid.
Mark. Two identical weights on a level beam, resting on a fulcrum with one brass point where it balances. Clear space equals one weight’s diameter. Minimum size 24px.
Never. Tilt the beam, make the weights differ, recolor the brass point, or place the mark over a busy photograph. The beam settles level once when it first appears.
