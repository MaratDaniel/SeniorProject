Where Adal is different.

Four formats on one engine

A seller picks the right way to sell each item, and the rules never change underneath.

Correctness we can test

Simultaneous bids are a test case, with a number to show, not a promise.

A second code at the purchase

Logging in is not enough. Finalizing a purchase asks for a fresh code.

Out of scope by design: real payments, identity checks (KYC), fraud scoring, native mobile apps.

One bid, one lock.

The row is lockedAny other bid on this auction waits its turn.

Three checksActive. Before the close on the server clock. Amount beats the price by the minimum increment (value).

Write and extendThe bid and price are stored. A bid near the end extends the close (length).

Commit, release, broadcastThe next bid is checked against the new price. Watchers are updated.
